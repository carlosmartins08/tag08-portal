# Consolidation risk register

This is a future-sanitization risk register, not a claim of current production failure. Each item distinguishes **ATUAL**, **ALVO APROVADO** and **GAP** where relevant.

| ID | Risk and state | Impact | Control before implementation |

| R2 SEO/publication coupling | **ATUAL:** route flags, locale readiness, evidence gate, sitemap and metadata independently affect publication. | Deindexing, duplicate canonical or accidental unpublished content exposure. | Snapshot sitemap/canonical/robots/status and validate public vs review modes. |

## Remaining gates

Before user-affecting waves, obtain BRM route dispositions (R3), preserve public evidence rules (R4), maintain editorial locale approval (R6), verify ClickUp list mapping/contact delivery (R7), confirm retention legal basis/schedule (R9), validate Railway worker/storage topology (R11), and protect current worktree boundaries (R17). CRM identity and target architecture are approved decisions, not unresolved questions; revisit only with objective infeasibility evidence.
